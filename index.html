Privacy Policy

EchoRoutine

Effective date: 22 July 2026

EchoRoutine ("the app") is a voice-guided routine, checklist, and timer app — built for shift workers, but usable by anyone who wants a spoken, hands-free schedule with workout timers and nutrition tracking. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Summary

EchoRoutine stores all of your data locally on your own device. The developer does not collect, receive, store, or have access to any of it. The app makes two kinds of outside network requests, both described below: a barcode lookup against the public Open Food Facts database, and — only if you choose to set one up — a direct call to an AI provider you've connected with your own API key.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Your routine schedule(s) and profiles (meal, water, workout, and study times you set or edit)
  • Checklist/completion state for routine items
  • Workout timer history (sets, reps, session summaries)
  • Food log entries and any custom foods you add
  • Body-weight log entries
  • Macro/nutrition targets and settings
  • Custom timers you create

None of this data is transmitted to the developer or to any third party, except where you explicitly use the barcode scanner, the optional AI routine generator, or the app's own export feature — all described below.

Barcode lookup (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a food barcode, the app sends the scanned barcode number to the public Open Food Facts API to retrieve that product's name and nutrition information. Only the barcode number is sent — no other information about you or your device is included in that request. This happens only when you actively use the scanner and have an internet connection; if it's unavailable, the app falls back to manual entry. Open Food Facts is a third-party, open database not operated by the developer — see their own privacy policy at world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

Optional AI routine generation (bring your own API key)

EchoRoutine can generate a routine, workout plan, or macro targets for you from a typed or spoken description, in two ways:

  • Copy a prompt, paste the AI's reply back — the app shows you a prompt template to copy into any AI chat app or website you already use (e.g. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini); you paste its JSON reply back into EchoRoutine to build your routine. The app itself sends nothing anywhere for this option.
  • Connect your own API key — if you optionally enter your own API key for an AI provider in Settings, the app will send your typed/spoken routine description directly to that provider's API, using your key, to get the routine JSON back automatically. Your API key is stored only on your device and is sent only to the provider it belongs to, over HTTPS — never to the developer. Requests made this way are governed by that provider's own privacy policy, not this one. You can remove a connected key at any time in Settings, which stops this feature until you add one again.

Export / Import

The app includes an optional, user-initiated export feature that saves your data as a JSON file to your device's storage (for backup, or to move data to a new phone) and an import feature to load such a file back in. This file is created and read only at your explicit request and is never automatically uploaded anywhere.

Permissions the app requests, and why

  • Camera — used only while the barcode scanner screen is open, to read food barcodes. The app does not take or store photos.
  • Notifications — used to announce workout/schedule cues (e.g. "drink water", "rest period over") if the app is in the background.
  • Wake lock / foreground service — keeps timers and voice cues accurate while the screen is off during a workout or scheduled routine.
  • Vibration — used for cue feedback alongside sound.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children.

Changes to this policy
